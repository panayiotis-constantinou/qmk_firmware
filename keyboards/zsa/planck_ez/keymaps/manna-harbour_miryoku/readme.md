# Planck EZ Miryoku

The Herdr MIDI control layer is shared with the Moonlander Miryoku keymap. See the [shared layer diagram](../../../../../users/manna-harbour_miryoku/readme.org#herdr-midi-control-layer).

Planck EZ-specific behavior:

- The six center and four bottom-outer positions are LED-only; controls use Miryoku's 36 physical switches.
- The center block's top two rows show the four agent slots (top left, top right, then middle left, middle right), and its bottom-left LED shows Scroll Lock.
- The inner bottom-left LED shows the sort mode: dim green for Herdr's Priority panel order, dim peach for Grouped.
- The space bar LED shows the host connection.
- The bottom-outer corners show Caps Lock (outer left) and the mouse jiggler (outer right).
- The two small LEDs below the space bar stay dark; they no longer show layers 1-3.
